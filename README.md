# AETHER — The Editorial Ghostwriter

> Transform your raw thoughts into polished, authoritative posts.

Aether is a premium, AI-powered writing assistant designed for professionals, founders, and creators who want to scale their personal brand without sounding like a robot. 

Instead of generating generic AI content, Aether acts as your personal editorial ghostwriter. You provide the raw thoughts, messy notes, or links—and Aether crafts them into high-performing, authoritative posts tailored specifically for your target platforms.

## Features

- **Multi-Platform Distribution:** Automatically adapt your thoughts for LinkedIn (storytelling), X (punchy & concise), and Threads (conversational).
- **Brand Voice Alignment:** Tailors the output to match your professional tone and common topics.
- **Intelligent Fallback:** Engineered with model resilience. If our primary AI provider (Gemini) experiences downtime, Aether seamlessly switches to our fallback model (Mistral) so you never miss a post.
- **Premium Aesthetics:** A distraction-free, "Aurora & Glass" inspired editorial interface designed to help you focus on what matters: the words.

## How it works

1. **Dump your thoughts:** Paste a link, write a random thought, or dump your raw meeting notes into the drafting editor.
2. **Select your channels:** Choose where you want to publish (LinkedIn, X, Threads).
3. **Generate & Review:** Aether ghostwrites the posts. Review the polished output, make any final tweaks, and publish.
